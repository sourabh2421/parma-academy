## 4. AEO & GEO Implementation Guidelines

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Guidelines:
1. **Direct-Answer Lead Paragraphs**: The first 1-2 sentences of every post must provide a factual, standalone answer to the core question being searched.
2. **Question-Structured Subheadings**: Structure all H2 and H3 headers as real questions asked by parents (e.g. *"What is the entry age for Nursery at Parma Academy?"* instead of *"Age Criteria"*).
3. **Structured Data FAQ Integration**: Every cornerstone article must include 3-5 high-value FAQs equipped with `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Guidelines:
1. **Named Entity Density**: Explicitly mention the primary entities: *"Parma Academy"*, *"Ayodhya"*, *"ICSE / CISCE board"*, *"Parikrama Marg"*, and *"Uttar Pradesh"*. Avoid ambiguous pronouns like "we" or "our school" when introducing facts so AI models (GPT-4o, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) extract facts correctly.
2. **Visible Freshness Markers**: Keep visible `Last Updated: [Date]` badges and `dateModified` in the JSON-LD schema.
3. **Pre-rendered HTML Delivery**: Ensure all content is embedded inside raw server-rendered/pre-rendered HTML so AI crawlers that do not execute JavaScript can extract full text instantaneously.
